A woman of substance

More than a name. A beautiful influence.

Her real name is Mrs. Blessing Lebechukwu Chioma, but to so many people, she is affectionately known as Mummy B. She is an expert in fashion, a teacher and a mentor whose impact is felt wherever she goes.

She has used her knowledge, creativity and experience to inspire others, nurture potential and make a meaningful difference. Her story is one of purpose, resilience, generosity and excellence.

At home, she shares life with her husband, Mr. Chinonso Lebechukwu Chidebelu, and their four outstanding children — a family that is part of her beautiful story.

With love and honour, Mummy B.
